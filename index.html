Calculation Formulas Applied:

  • Gross Salary = Basic Salary + HRA + DA
  • Total Deductions = PF + Income Tax
  • Net Take-Home Salary = Gross Salary − Total Deductions (Rounded using Math.round())

Syllabus Concepts Applied (Units 1 to 5)

Unit 1

OOP Basics, Constructors, Math, Arrays

Classes Employee and Department, parameterized constructors, Math.round() for currency precision, and breakdown component arrays.

Unit 2

Inheritance, Polymorphism & Interface

Interface Payable with overriding calculateSalary() in subclasses Manager, Clerk, and Teacher inside package payroll.model.

Unit 3

Custom Exception & JavaBean Standards

Custom checked exception InvalidSalaryException thrown when basic salary ≤ 0. JavaBean conventions for private fields and getters/setters.

Unit 4

JDBC CRUD & ExecuteQuery Reporting

PreparedStatement CRUD operations in EmployeeDAO, and Statement.executeQuery() for department aggregate reports in SalaryDAO.

Unit 5

Swing GUI Forms, Layouts & Event Handling

JTextField, JLabel, JButton forms with GridLayout, JTable visualization, and ActionListener event handling across 5 tabbed panels in MainFrame.
